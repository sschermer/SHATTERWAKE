# SHATTERWAKE hero fighter – attribution and source
The prototype master and upgrade modules are original project-authored Blender
geometry and original procedural metal materials. No third-party model, font,
texture or external artwork is copied into this prototype. The old Grab3D ship
in the Android main branch is unrelated and was rejected as the target design.

Future third-party CC0 materials must be individually listed here with their
original creator, exact URL and license before inclusion. Voluntary credit
is required by SHATTERWAKE project policy even for CC0 sources.

The resulting art remains on a non-production branch until explicit approval.
