# SHATTERWAKE: playable prototype design (0.3.0-alpha)

**Genre:** native Android portrait shooter with vertical scrolling and pseudo-isometric, procedurally drawn spaceships, asteroid objects and shadows. Designed for small touchscreens; no cloud services, no remote assets.

### Determined design choices

- Primary blaster fires automatically; within +-15 degrees it corrects toward a visible enemy or individual surviving boss module. Targeting is preferentially center-biased, not automatic global lock-on.
- Controls: (1) single finger to move ship; arm MISSILE/EMP by tapping its icon, release moving finger to discharge; (2) two-finger profile with direct weapon buttons. Both in SETTINGS. Primary continues firing without touch.
- Two secondary weapons: homing missiles and EMP. Both use charges regenerated by combat and passage of time.
- Four on-demand ability buttons: SHIELD, RAPID, TRIPLE and PLASMA; charges drop from enemies or rare modules, with timed effects.
- Stars drift down periodically and come from enemies and boss modules. Collect physically. Star magnet is upgradeable.
- Defeat modular bosses to reach the star shipyard. Every star purchase (fire-rate 5 levels; damage; repair; magnet; drone; shield; ability charges) persists for **one run**, not across runs.
- Permanently unlock tactical technology entries by defeating bosses (stored locally) but never permanently increase starting attack power.
- Genuine branching paths: stage 1 junkyard worm boss; stage 2 either machine colony fortress or nebula prism leviathan; stage 3 either storm hive or Null Citadel architect. Five distinct scenes/boss structures, three stages per full run. Encounter waves and special offers vary.
- Up to two continues at death; each costs **half** of current remaining stars rounded up, keeps shop upgrades and returns hull to full.
- Boss modular anatomy: weapon nodes, connecting nodes, cargo and reactor nodes; destroyable parts lead to rewards, changed attacks and a splitting fragment in worm fight. Bosses have changing phases.

### Early-game balance targets (0.3 alpha)

- Enemy fire is disabled during the initial 18 seconds of sector 1 to let players learn movement.
- The first 20 seconds permit at most 3 concurrent enemies, increasing in tiers to 8 later in sector 1.
- First-stage enemy shots are capped at 3 active projectiles through second 55, and capped at 5 through second 90.
- Enemies appear more slowly, one at a time in opening waves, with reduced velocity and hull strength.
- Starter hull remains 6; damage invulnerability adjusted to 2 seconds.
- Boss salvo density reduced, with slower projectiles, fewer volleys and longer pauses.
- These are *tuning assumptions*, not yet validated by measured phone playtests.

### Boss transition (0.3 alpha)

1. Stop new enemy and ambient star spawns at sector timer threshold.
2. Let remaining enemies naturally exit or be defeated; no teleporting bosses onto the ship.
3. Clear remaining hostile shots, blink a centered warning and play an original three-second synthesized siren.
4. Animate every boss node from fully offscreen at the top over 3.7 seconds.
5. Allow aiming, firing, boss collisions and attacks only after the fly-in; grant 1.3 seconds of brief protection.

### Star economy (0.3 alpha)

- Enemy and modular boss star bursts scaled to about 55% of prior values.
- Ambient star supply reduced to 11–14 per drop every 6.7–9.5 seconds.
- Boss bonuses reduced; shop prices for higher weapon levels and support systems increased.
- Prices are heuristic first-pass balance, not measured device-playtest figures.

### Explicit prototype compromises / next iteration

1. Canvas geometry is stylized pseudo-isometric rather than a full 3D engine. This is intentional and commercially license-simple.
2. Control feel, balancing, collision boxes, game speed, screen layout and effects are first-pass parameters **not validated on a real Android device yet**.
3. Route choice selects worlds and bosses; deeper persistent battlefield-echo from one world to the next is reserved for later.
4. Boss segments and independent fragment enemy operate in simplified logic; true articulated physics / sophisticated enemy AI remain future work.
5. Persistent save currently covers only best score, unlocked tech and preferences, **not** an in-progress run. Menu return discards the run.
6. No ads, purchases, analytics, cloud backup, multiplayer or online leaderboards are implemented.

### Player controls and UI

- Start: NEUER DURCHLAUF.
- Move: touch/drag within the central area. Ship follows with offset to avoid jumping under the finger.
- Top right: pause.
- Bottom right: missile/EMP selection or firing (depending on mode).
- Bottom abilities: tap to activate shield/rapid/triple/plasma when charged.
- Shop: tap purchase cards; WEITER to branching navigation (or finish at last sector).
- At death: choose continue for half your stars (max two) or finish run.
