## Voluntary artist credits for future CC0 artwork

**SHATTERWAKE credits creators voluntarily even when CC0-1.0 does not require
attribution.** Once any external 3D model, PBR texture, HDRI, rendered image or
other asset is actually integrated, record its *specific* original filename or
asset ID, the individual artist (if named), source URL, license and any changes.
Keep credits with this repository and with distribution documentation. The
SHATTERWAKE proprietary project license does not supersede the original CC0
dedication of those assets.

## Current 3D fighter assets in Android (original work)

The currently included player craft and upgrade modules are original
SHATTERWAKE Blender geometry, procedurally generated by the project from
`art/hero_fighter/build_master.py`. Its source master is preserved on the
`art/hero-fighter-v1` branch. The Android build contains only baked transparent
PNG renders in GAME and WERFT views, including an independently rendered
industrial hangar background. The same original scene produced all sprite
layers; it contains no external 3D models, textures, photo assets or
third-party concept art.

**Creator:** SHATTERWAKE project (project-authored geometry and materials).
**Source:** https://github.com/sschermer/SHATTERWAKE/tree/art/hero-fighter-v1/art/hero_fighter
**License:** SHATTERWAKE project `LICENSE`. This is **not** a claim that the
images generated from project-authored geometry were released under CC0.

**Historical asset, no longer used in the Android game:**
Grab3D – Spaceship, CC0 1.0
(https://grab3d.com/models/sci-fi/spaceship/).
Its external 3D model and old sprites were replaced in this version.
We retain this voluntary historical acknowledgement but do **not** misrepresent
the retired work as bundled in the current APK.

## Commercial-release audit obligations

- Recalculate the runtime dependency inventory (including transitives) after **every** addition of a library or asset, including artwork, effects, music, fonts and code snippets.
- Keep copyright holder, license URL, version, attribution and distribution conditions for each added component; copy license and NOTICE text into the distribution where required.
- Confirm rights for the *specific recording*, *specific image*, *specific font*, etc. Publicly viewable or royalty-free is **not** automatically permission to sell a game that contains it.
- Open-source components can have patent, copyleft and NOTICE obligations. Obtain a distribution-appropriate professional review before release.
- Do not reuse online images displayed during early concept discussions; **none are in this project**.
- Clear the `SHATTERWAKE` name and game logo for relevant markets and classes before advertising or distribution.
